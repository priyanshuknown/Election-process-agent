Document Guide

Make sure you have the right ID before heading to the polling booth.

Accepted Documents

You must carry any ONE of these original documents to vote:

  • Voter ID Card (EPIC) The primary proof of registration.
  • Aadhaar Card Widely accepted alternative ID.
  • Driving License Smart card or booklet format.
  • PAN Card Permanent Account Number card.
  • Indian Passport Valid passport booklet.

Not Accepted

These documents will NOT be accepted at the polling booth:

  • Photos on Phone Digital photos of ID cards are invalid.
  • Photocopies Only original documents are verified.
  • Utility Bills Electricity, water, or phone bills.
  • Bank Passbooks without Photo Passbooks must have an attested photo.
Important Note

Your name on the ID must match the name on the electoral roll. Minor spelling mistakes are usually ignored if your identity can be established.
