Your Voting Journey

Follow these steps to become a proud voter.

0% Completed

1. Check Eligibility

Before you begin, make sure you legally qualify to vote in India.

  • Must be an Indian Citizen.
  • Must be 18 years or older on the qualifying date (typically Jan 1st).
  • Must be a resident of the polling area where you wish to enroll.
Check My Eligibility Now

2. Register as Voter (Form 6)

You need to register your name in the Electoral Roll by filling out Form 6.

  1. Visit the Official Voter Portal (voters.eci.gov.in).
  2. Create an account using your mobile number.
  3. Select "Fill Form 6" for new voter registration.
  4. Upload your photo, address proof (Aadhaar/Passport), and age proof.
  5. Submit and note down your reference number to track the status.

3. Obtain Voter ID (EPIC)

Once your Form 6 is approved, an EPIC (Electors Photo Identity Card) number is generated.

  • Digital Copy: You can download your e-EPIC immediately from the portal once approved.
  • Physical Copy: The physical PVC card will be delivered to your registered address via Speed Post.

4. Find Your Polling Booth

A few days before the election, confirm exactly where you need to go.

  • Check your "Part Number" and "Serial Number" on the electoral roll.
  • Locate your designated polling station.
Use Booth Finder

5. Voting Day!

It's time to exercise your democratic right.

  1. Carry your original Voter ID (or an approved alternate document).
  2. Stand in line at your booth. Polling officials will check your ID.
  3. They will mark your finger with indelible ink.
  4. Proceed to the EVM (Electronic Voting Machine).
  5. Press the blue button next to your chosen candidate. Wait for the red light and the "beep" sound!
